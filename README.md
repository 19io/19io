## About 19 IO

19 IO HOST works across several areas of the digital infrastructure ecosystem, including domain registration and management, web hosting, reseller infrastructure, VPS solutions, software licensing, server administration, digital services, and API-based projects.

Our focus is on making reliable infrastructure and accessible technology available to businesses at different stages of growth. Alongside our hosting and domain services, we continue to develop digital platforms and technology projects.
